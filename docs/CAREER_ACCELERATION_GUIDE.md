SECTION 5: INTERVIEW PREPARATION (START IMMEDIATELY)
================================================================================

When you get interviews (you will), be prepared to:

1. LIVE CODING DEMONSTRATION:
   - Be ready to run your model live
   - Show PMF generation in real-time
   - Explain technical decisions
   - Handle questions about implementation details

2. TECHNICAL QUESTIONS YOU'LL GET:

   • "Why use ensemble methods instead of a single model?"
   → Answer: Diversity of predictions, reduced overfitting, captures different
   patterns, meta-learner finds optimal weights
   
   • "How do you handle model calibration?"
   → Answer: Isotonic regression on out-of-sample predictions, track calibration
   plots, continuous recalibration
   
   • "What's your approach to feature engineering?"
   → Answer: Domain-specific (matchup, rest, usage), derived features (moving
   averages, momentum), interaction terms, player-specific patterns
   
   • "How do you quantify uncertainty?"
   → Answer: Prediction intervals from cross-validation, distribution fitting,
   variance in ensemble predictions, empirical error analysis
   
   • "Explain your margin-building approach."
   → Answer: (This is your differentiator - explain all 4 methods in detail)
   
   • "How would you scale this to 1000+ players and 10+ prop types?"
   → Answer: Parallel training, model caching, API design, real-time data
   pipeline, cloud infrastructure
   
   • "What's your backtesting strategy?"
   → Answer: Walk-forward validation, expanding window, out-of-time testing,
   Kelly criterion for bet sizing, drawdown analysis

3. BUSINESS QUESTIONS YOU'LL GET:

   • "How would you identify +EV opportunities?"
   → Compare your probabilities to market lines, look for > 3% edge, consider
   liquidity and limits, track CLV (closing line value)
   
   • "How do you handle line movement?"
   → Sharp vs public money indicators, reverse line movement, steam moves, track
   your predictions vs market close
   
   • "What's your expected ROI?"
   → Be conservative: "5-8% ROI on <3% Kelly bets over large sample size"
   
   • "How much volume can this model handle?"
   → "Current implementation: all NBA games, all major props, 1000+ predictions
   per day. Can scale horizontally with proper infrastructure."

4. PREPARE QUESTIONS TO ASK THEM:

   • "What's your current modeling approach for player props?"
   • "What tools and data sources does your team use?"
   • "How do you measure analyst performance?"
   • "What's the typical path for growth on your trading desk?"
   • "How much autonomy would I have in implementing new methodologies?"
   • "What's your tech stack?"
   • "Do you have dedicated ML infrastructure?"
