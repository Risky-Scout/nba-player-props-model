Pricing FrameworkMarket Maker Mathematics
Fair Odds — Our Line
p ≥ 0.5 → −100·p/(1−p)
p < 0.5 → +100·(1−p)/p
Converts calibrated model probability to the American odds we would post as the book. When our Fair Odds differ from the Posted Odds, a pricing gap exists and an edge can be captured.
Vig Removal
p_true = p_over / (p_over + p_under)
Strips bookmaker margin from both sides of the market to expose the fair implied probability. Required before any edge calculation is valid.
Edge & Expected Value
edge = p_model − (1/decimal)
EV = (d−1)·p − (1−p)
Edge quantifies the pricing gap vs market. EV is the economic return per unit wagered — the true measure of long-run profitability at any odds.
Kelly Criterion
f* = (b·p − q) / b
b = net decimal − 1, p = model win prob, q = 1−p. Optimal fractional wager that maximizes long-run wealth growth rate. Shown per row, capped at 25%.
