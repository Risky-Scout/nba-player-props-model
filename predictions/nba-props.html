Pricing FrameworkMarket Maker Mathematics
Fair Odds — Our Line
p ≥ 0.5 → −100·p/(1−p)
p < 0.5 → +100·(1−p)/p
Maps model probability to its equivalent American odds representation — the theoretically fair price with zero margin. Divergence between this fair price and the posted market price defines the pricing gap the model is measuring.
Vig Removal
p_true = p_over / (p_over + p_under)
Raw implied probabilities from a two-sided market sum to greater than 1.0 due to the bookmaker's margin. Normalizing both sides recovers the market's true probability estimate, which is the correct baseline for any model comparison.
Edge & Expected Value
edge = p_model − (1/decimal)
EV = (d−1)·p − (1−p)
Edge is the difference between model probability and the market's vig-removed implied probability. EV expresses this difference in return-per-unit terms, weighting the magnitude of the odds. Both are required to assess line quality — edge alone does not account for price.
Kelly Criterion
f* = (b·p − q) / b
b = decimal odds − 1, p = model probability, q = 1 − p. Derived from maximizing the expected logarithm of wealth, Kelly sizing is the theoretically optimal fraction of capital to allocate given a known probability estimate and price. Output is capped at 25% per position.
SGP Correlation
ρ(PTS,REB)=0.647 · ρ(PTS,AST)=0.426
ρ(PTS,3PM)=0.512 · ρ(REB,AST)=0.274
Same-game parlay pricing requires joint probability estimation across correlated outcomes. Naive multiplication of independent probabilities overstates true joint probability. These empirical correlations, derived from historical NBA box scores, are used to adjust the Gaussian copula model's joint distribution accordingly.
