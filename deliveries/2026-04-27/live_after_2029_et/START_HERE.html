Honest contract

  • The canonical PMFs in MODEL_ONLY files are the standalone model: active-conditioned + role-aware calibrated; FG3M additionally has a validated k≥7, w=0.2 tail-shrink correction.
  • Market columns (line, market_fair_over_prob, market_source, market_offered_*, model_edge_vs_market) are comparison references only. They do not modify pmf_json.
  • The MARKET_ANCHORED_REFERENCE files have a market-tilted pmf_json (CDF anchored at the offered line). They are not for evaluating the standalone model.
  • The latest matched closing-line audit found the de-vigged closing market beats the standalone calibrated model on log-loss in 9 of 11 cohorts (95% CI). No market-beating claim is made.
